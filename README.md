# 🌾 KisanMitr

### Connecting Farmers with Better Market Opportunities

KisanMitr is a technology-driven platform designed to help farmers
make better decisions about selling their crops and accessing
useful agricultural information.

## 🎯 Problem

Farmers often face challenges such as:

- Lack of transparent market price information
- Dependence on intermediaries
- Difficulty finding better selling opportunities
- Lack of timely information about demand and weather
- Difficulty discovering relevant government schemes

## 💡 Our Solution

KisanMitr brings useful farming and market information together
in one simple platform.

### 🚀 Key Features

- 📊 Smart crop price recommendations
- 📈 Mandi price and market trend comparison
- 🌦️ Weather-aware crop suggestions
- 🔔 Government scheme and subsidy alerts
- 🚚 Transport sharing
- 📅 Best-selling-time alerts
- 🌐 Regional language support

## 🛠️ Technology

The project is being developed using modern web and AI technologies.

> Tech stack will be updated as development progresses.

## 🌱 Future Scope

- 🎤 Voice-based farmer assistance
- 📱 Offline support
- 🌾 Crop quality detection
- 🤖 AI-powered agricultural consultation
- 💰 Profit calculation and market comparison

## 👥 Team

Developed as a student innovation/hackathon project.

## 🎯 Vision

To make useful agricultural information easier to access and
help farmers make more informed decisions.
